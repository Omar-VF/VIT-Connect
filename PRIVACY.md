# Privacy Policy for VIT-Connect

**Last updated:** July 26, 2026

**VIT-Connect** ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how user data is handled by the extension.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
- **No Remote Data Collection:** VIT-Connect does **NOT** collect, transmit, track, or store any personal data, usage analytics, or credentials on any external servers, databases, or third-party services.
- **Local Credential Storage:** Your login credentials (username and password) are stored **locally** on your device using Chrome's secure `chrome.storage.sync` API. They are exclusively used to automatically authenticate with the VIT Vellore captive portal (`phc.prontonetworks.com`) when connected to the hostel Wi-Fi.

## 2. Data Sharing and Third Parties
- We do **NOT** sell, trade, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties.
- Credentials are sent directly to the local captive portal authentication gateway on your network.

## 3. Data Control and Deletion
You can remove your saved credentials at any time by editing or clearing them in the extension popup menu, or by uninstalling the extension from your browser.

## 4. Contact
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository:
